Encampment Response Policy — Downtown Core
Decision date: Jun 14, 2026 (target) Steward: D. Okonkwo Issue: Downtown Homelessness Response
1
Decision Statement
Complete
The city will adopt a housing-first encampment response protocol for the downtown core. All encampment clearings must be preceded by a minimum 72-hour outreach window with confirmed shelter or housing offers. SFPD enforcement actions are conditioned on DPH outreach team sign-off. The DPH will track placement rates and report monthly to the Board of Supervisors.

Options Considered

Selected Housing-first with 72-hr outreach window
Chosen because it aligns enforcement authority with service delivery, has the strongest evidence base, and directly addresses the causal claim about downstream health costs. Addresses dissent from BID around enforcement timeline by guaranteeing a response window.
Not selected Enforcement-first with voluntary shelter referral
Rejected: evidence from outreach interviews indicates voluntary referrals without guaranteed placement have a <15% uptake rate. Does not address the downstream health cost causal claim.
Not selected Status quo — case-by-case SFPD discretion
Rejected: no systematic data collection, no accountability mechanism, and inconsistent outcomes across neighborhoods. Cannot generate the indicator data needed for adaptive governance.
