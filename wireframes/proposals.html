Housing-first encampment response with 72-hr outreach window
Submitted by D. Okonkwo (Outreach Workers) Jun 1, 2026 Revision 3
Under Review
Proposal Summary

The city should adopt a housing-first encampment response protocol across the downtown core. All encampment clearings must be preceded by a minimum 72-hour outreach engagement period, during which the DPH outreach team must confirm the availability of a shelter bed or transitional housing offer before SFPD can proceed.

SFPD enforcement actions on encampments are conditioned on DPH outreach team authorization. The DPH will maintain placement records and report monthly to the Board of Supervisors on offer rates, placement rates, and 90-day housing stability outcomes.

This proposal directly addresses the contested causal claim that clearing without housing placement increases downstream health costs and recidivism, by structurally tying enforcement to housing availability.

K. Petrov — SFPD Operational

The causal claim underlying this proposal conflates correlation and causation. The 68% SFPD offer rate has not been validated as equivalent to actual placements — the factual basis is contested.
