Issue Description Edit

How should the city respond to rising homelessness in the downtown core? This issue involves tensions between emergency response and structural housing investment, between enforcement and harm-reduction approaches, and between short-term public order and long-term systems change.

Issue Summary

This issue centers on a fundamental tension: enforcement approaches that address visible homelessness in the short term vs. structural investment that addresses it sustainably. Sources show broad agreement that shelter capacity is insufficient, but deep disagreement on the right response — split largely along stakeholder lines that also reflect values and identity, not just evidence.

Alerts
⚠ Missing voices Unhoused residents and hospital discharge planners are referenced frequently but have minimal direct testimony. Decisions made without these perspectives carry legitimacy risk.
